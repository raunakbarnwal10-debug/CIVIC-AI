Why Civic AI?

Citizen feedback can be difficult to organize when hundreds of requests are submitted across various channels without categorization. Local administrative bodies often struggle with unstructured messages, misplaced complaints, and delayed response times.

Civic AI provides a simple, intelligent digital layer that empowers both citizens and governance bodies:

📥

Collect Citizen Complaints

Clean, accessible natural-language reporting for ordinary citizens.

🗂️

Categorize Problems

Automatic classification into Road, Sanitation, Water, Electricity, and Education.

🚨

Identify Priority

Immediate detection of high-risk public safety hazards near schools and hospitals.

🏛️

Suggest Departments

Instant jurisdictional routing to Municipal PWD, Jal Board, or Electricity boards.
